CHAPTER II

WHY +33

+33 is the international dialing code of France.

To most people, it is a number.

To us, it is an invitation.

A bridge between cultures.

A reminder that beauty can travel beyond geography.

II. DESIGN WITH SOUL

III. HOSPITALITY AS ART
